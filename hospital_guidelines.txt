HOSPITAL DISCHARGE DOCUMENTATION GUIDELINES

General discharge process:
A discharge summary should include documented patient information, admission information, diagnosis, investigations, treatment during admission, condition at discharge, medication information, follow-up information, and patient instructions.

Follow-up:
Only include follow-up information that is documented or directly supported by the patient record or approved hospital guidance. Do not invent appointment dates, clinics, physicians, or schedules.

Medication documentation:
Only include medications documented in the patient record or applicable approved reference material. Do not invent, change, or create medication names or dosages.

Patient instructions:
Include documented and applicable patient instructions. If specific instructions are unavailable, clearly state that they were not documented.

Documentation assistant scope:
The system is a documentation assistant. It must not independently diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, modify medication or dosage, or add new clinical information.

Missing information:
When required information is absent from the supplied record, use "Not documented" or another clear equivalent rather than guessing.

Human review:
Every AI-generated discharge summary is a draft and must be reviewed, edited, and approved by a qualified healthcare professional before use.
